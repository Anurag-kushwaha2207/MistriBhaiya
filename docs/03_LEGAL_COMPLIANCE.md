# Legal / Compliance checklist (CA aur lawyer se confirm karwayein)

> Ye sirf jaankari hai, legal salah nahi. Rules badalte rehte hain.

## 1. Company aur tax
- Pvt Ltd / LLP, GST registration (platform fee par GST lagta hai), PAN/TAN, current account.
- DPIIT recognition (aapki startup pehle se recognised ho to us ka fayda lein).

## 2. Code on Social Security (gig / platform workers)
- Research me mila: platform "aggregator" ko **turnover ka 1-2% (workers ko diye gaye payment ke 5% tak cap)** social security fund me dena pad sakta hai, portal par registration, aur workers ki onboarding/exit ki reporting.
- **Kya aapka construction-labour marketplace "aggregator" ki definition me aata hai?** Ye is par depend karta hai ki sarkar ne kaunsi categories notify ki hain. **Lawyer se pata karein** aur design me workers ka data (e-Shram UAN optional field) pehle se rakhein.

## 3. Data privacy: DPDP Act 2023 aur Rules
- Phased compliance; poori compliance ki tareekh lagbhag **mid-2027** batayi gayi hai (verify karein).
- Zaroori: consent notice (Hindi me), kam se kam data lena, data security, breach report, data hatane ka tareeka, **18 saal se kam ke users ko onboard na karein**.
- Is code me: mistri ka exact ghar ka location customer ko kabhi nahi bheja jata (sirf doori + gaon).
- **Aadhaar number store na karein.** Verification ke liye DigiLocker / authorised KYC partner use karein.

## 4. Labour aur safety
- Child labour (18 se kam) bilkul nahi. Height/electric/construction kaam me safety guidance aur (ho sake to) accident insurance partner.
- Mazdoori tay karne me minimum wage ka dhyan; platform "rate suggest" kare, force nahi.

## 5. Calling aur SMS (TRAI / DLT)
- OTP/SMS ke liye **DLT registration** (entity + template) zaroori hai.
- Masked calling ke liye telecom provider (Exotel, Knowlarity, MyOperator, etc.) ke terms; call recording par consent.

## 6. Terms, Privacy Policy, Grievance Officer
- Terms of use (platform sirf jodta hai, kaam ki guarantee/mistri ka employer nahi), refund/dispute policy, Hindi + English me.
- Grievance officer ka naam aur contact app me dikhaana.

## 7. Payment
- Khud paisa rakhna (escrow) RBI rules me aa sakta hai. Payment aggregator (Razorpay/Cashfree) ke through karein, aur nodal/escrow ka structure unse samjhein.
