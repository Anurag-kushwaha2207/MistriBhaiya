# Paise kamane ke tareeke (continuous income)

> Neeche ke numbers **assumptions** hain (research se tay nahi). Pehle 2 zile ka asli data aane do, phir badalna.

## Kamai ke 7 raste (kam risk se zyada tak)
1. **Booking fee (ghar-malik se):** har confirmed booking par chhoti fee (jaise ₹29-99). Sabse seedha.
2. **Mistri Pro subscription:** priority listing, verified badge, zyada jobs ka alert (jaise ₹99-199/month). Free tier hamesha rakhein.
3. **Calling credit / masked call:** log app ke bahar deal na karein, isliye call app ke through; call ka kharcha fee me shaamil.
4. **Contractor SaaS:** attendance, mazdoor ka hisaab, advance, GST invoice (jaise ₹499-1,999/month).
5. **Material marketplace commission:** cement, sariya, ret, tiles. Margin supplier se negotiate.
6. **Ads / local hardware dukaan ki listing:** hardware shops, paint dealers ko "aas-paas" me dikhna.
7. **Partnerships:** tool loan, insurance, skilling certificate (partner ke through, regulated kaam khud na karein).

## Ek simple hisaab (sirf samajhne ke liye)
Maan lijiye 1 zila me **mahine ki 3,000 confirmed bookings**, **₹49 booking fee**:
- Booking fee = 3,000 x ₹49 = **₹1,47,000/month**
- 300 mistri "Pro" x ₹99 = ₹29,700/month
- 20 contractor x ₹999 = ₹19,980/month
- Kul lagbhag **₹1.97 lakh/month per zila** (assumption).
- Kharcha: SMS/OTP, hosting, calls, 5-10 Mitra, support, marketing. Isliye pehle 3-5 zile me **unit economics** (har booking par kitna bacha) pakka karein.

## Calling kharcha ka dhyan
Ek provider ne masked calling ~₹1.5/min batayi thi (verify karein). 2 minute ki call = ~₹3-4. Isliye:
- Phase 1: direct call (tel:). Phase 2: masked call, aur booking fee se kharcha cover.

## Kaise "continuous" bane (sirf ek baar ka paisa nahi)
- **Repeat:** ghar banane me hafto/mahino mistri chahiye. Ek ghar-malik kai baar book karta hai.
- **Subscription** (mistri Pro, contractor SaaS) = recurring.
- **Material** = bada ticket size, baar-baar.
- Pehle supply (mistri) pakki karein, phir demand.

## Takkar ka jawab (differentiation)
1. Live "free/busy" (auto-expire + auto-busy on booking) = aaj ka kaam, aaj ka bharosa.
2. Customizable doori (ghar-malik bhi, mistri bhi).
3. Gaon-first: Hindi/voice, missed-call, Mitra network, chowk.
4. Trust: rating, verified badge, kaam ki photo.
